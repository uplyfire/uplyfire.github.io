Reporting a bug

The fastest way to get a fix is to include:

  • your device model and Android version,
  • the app version (Settings screen shows it),
  • what you did, what you expected, and what happened instead,
  • a screenshot if the problem is visual.

Common quick fixes

  • Map loads slowly the first time - the map is cached after first launch; later starts are faster and work offline.
  • Bought Pro on a new phone? - Pro is tied to your Google account; reinstalling or switching devices restores it automatically via Google Play.
