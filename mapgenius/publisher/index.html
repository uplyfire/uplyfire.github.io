MapGenius Publisher

MapGenius Publisher is our internal, first-party tool. It publishes the game's own promotional videos (the daily "find the country in 5 seconds" series) to our own social media accounts and reads their basic statistics. It serves no third-party users, has no public sign-up, and processes no data of anyone other than our own accounts.

What it does

  • Uploads our finished episode videos to our own channels on a daily schedule.
  • Adds our own captions, covers and first comments.
  • Reads view/like/comment counts of our own posts to build a daily report.
  • Helps us answer comments on our own posts.

YouTube API Services

MapGenius Publisher uses YouTube API Services to manage our own channel's videos - see our Privacy Policy (Section 17, incl. the Google Privacy Policy) and Terms & Conditions; use of the tool is also governed by the YouTube Terms of Service.

Other platform APIs

The tool also uses the official APIs of Instagram, Threads and Facebook (Meta) and TikTok's Display API, in each case exclusively to publish to and read statistics of accounts we own. Authentication always happens through each platform's own official login flow; the tool never stores anyone's passwords.
