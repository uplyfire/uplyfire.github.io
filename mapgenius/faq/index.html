FAQ

Is MapGenius free?

Yes. The game is free to play and supported by ads. An optional one-time Pro purchase removes the limits permanently - there is no subscription.

Which platforms is it on?

Android, on Google Play. An iOS version is in the works.

Does it work offline?

Yes - the map and quizzes work without an internet connection.

What languages does it support?

12 languages. The interface and place names follow your choice, so you can learn geography in the language you think in.

How much content is there?

All 243 countries and territories, plus 587+ other places across 12 quiz categories: capitals, flags, rivers, mountains, monuments, islands, lakes, deserts and waterfalls. Playable worldwide or per continent, at 5 difficulty levels.

Is it multiple choice?

No. Every answer is a tap on a real, zoomable map. That is the whole idea.

I keep failing the hard levels. Any advice?

Open Study Mode first - the same map with names revealed. Five minutes of study usually beats an hour of guessing. Hints are there for the stubborn rounds.

What is the daily 5-second challenge?

A short video series we post daily as @mapgenius (TikTok), @mapgenius.app (Instagram, Threads) and @mapgenius-app (YouTube): one country, five seconds, no labels. The answer drops the next day.

How do you handle my data?

The short version: we keep it minimal. The full version is in the Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions.

I found a bug / my question is not here.

Write to uplyfire@gmail.com - see the Support page for what to include.
