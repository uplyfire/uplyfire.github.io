How to play

1. Pick what to play Choose a quiz category — countries, capitals, flags, rivers, mountains and more — and optionally narrow it down to one continent. Not sure yet? "All games" lists every mode in one place.
2. Pick how hard Five difficulty levels. Level 1 is a warm-up; level 5 is for people who know where Kiribati is without blinking.
3. Tap the map Each question names a place. You answer by tapping it on a real, zoomable world map — no multiple choice, no lists to pick from. The closer you are, the better.
4. Stuck? Hint or skip A hint narrows the search down; a skip moves you on. Both are honest about the score, so a clean run still means something.
5. See how you did After each round you get your result and it feeds your statistics and rankings, per category — so you can watch your accuracy climb over time.

Want to learn before you compete?

Study Mode shows the map with everything labeled, so you can explore first and quiz yourself later. It is the fastest way to turn "I think it's somewhere north" into a first-try hit.

The daily 5-second challenge

On social media we run one round a day: a short video shows an unlabeled map and gives you 5 seconds to find the country. The answer is revealed the next day in the same thread.
