Features

One rule, one map

MapGenius never shows multiple-choice buttons. Every question is answered the same way: you look at a real, interactive world map and tap where you think the answer is. It is harder than it sounds — and that is the point.

What you can be quizzed on

🌍 Countries — all 243 countries & territories
🏛️ Capitals
🚩 Flags
🏞️ Rivers
⛰️ Mountains
🗿 Monuments
🏝️ Islands
💧 Lakes
🏜️ Deserts
🌊 Waterfalls

12 quiz categories in total, over 587 places beyond the countries themselves. You can play the whole world or focus on a single continent.

Difficulty that actually scales

  • 5 difficulty levels — from "everyone knows France" to places that stump geography teachers.
  • Hints and skips when you are stuck, so a hard round never turns into a wall.

Learn, then compete

  • Study Mode — explore the map with names revealed before you take a quiz.
  • Statistics — your accuracy and progress per category over time.
  • Rankings — compare your scores and chase your own records.

Make it yours

  • 12 map themes — from classic atlas looks to dark mode.
  • 12 languages — play and learn in the language you think in.
  • Offline play — the map and quizzes work without a connection.

Free, with a fair upgrade

MapGenius is free to play. A single, one-time Pro purchase unlocks everything permanently — there is no subscription and no recurring charge.
